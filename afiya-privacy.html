Afiya Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Afiya is a personal wellness app built by Zahra El Hamraoui. It reads your Oura Ring data to give you one daily recommendation. This policy explains what data Afiya uses and where it goes.

Data Afiya reads from Oura

When you connect your Oura account, you choose to share these scopes: personal info, daily summaries (sleep, readiness, activity, stress, temperature), heart rate, workouts, sessions and SpO2. Afiya only reads this data; it never writes to your Oura account.

Where your data is stored

Afiya has no server and no account system. Your Oura data is fetched directly from Oura to your device. Your Oura access token is kept in your device's secure storage. Your profile, cycle information and saved advice are stored only on your device.

AI recommendations (optional)

If you add your own Anthropic API key, Afiya sends a summary of today's data to Anthropic's Claude API to generate your recommendation: first name, cycle phase, sleep, heart rate, HRV, readiness, activity, stress, SpO2, temperature, recent workouts, goals and hormonal profile. Anthropic's handling of that data is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy. If you don't add a key, nothing is sent.

What Afiya does not do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone else.
  • No collection of your data by the developer.

Deleting your data

In the app, Profile → Disconnect revokes Afiya's access to Oura. Profile → Delete all my data erases everything Afiya stored on your device. You can also revoke access anytime from your Oura account settings.

Changes
